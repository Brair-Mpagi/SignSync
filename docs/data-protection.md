# Data protection: what the code enforces

Uganda's **Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019** and its 2021 Regulations govern everything this
project records: video and audio of identifiable individuals, plus landmark data derived from them.
Plan §16 sets the policy. This document records how that policy is enforced **in code**, so that
compliance does not depend on everyone remembering the rules.

## The rule

> No clip enters training, evaluation, export, or a demo without a consent record that is present,
> unexpired, un-withdrawn, and that covers the specific use.

`signsync.datasets.consent.ConsentRegistry` implements this. `Corpus.load()` routes every clip
through it. A clip lacking valid consent raises `ConsentError` — it is not silently skipped, because
a silent skip hides a compliance failure just as effectively as it hides a data-loss bug.

## Consent scopes

Consent is not a boolean. A participant may agree to some uses and not others, and the registry
models each separately:

| Scope | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `training` | Landmarks/video may be used to train models |
| `evaluation` | May be used in held-out evaluation sets |
| `publication` | Stills or clips may appear in papers, talks, demos |
| `open_release` | May be included in a public research corpus (plan §17) |
| `commercial` | May support a commercially deployed service |

Absent scopes default to **denied**. `open_release` and `commercial` in particular must never be
inferred from a general research consent — plan §16.3 requires that downstream commercial value be
settled with UNAD and participants up front.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal is retroactive by design: setting `withdrawn_on` removes the participant's clips from
every subsequent load, and `signsync.datasets.consent` provides the audit query needed to identify
derived artefacts (splits, trained checkpoints) that must be regenerated. Withdrawal is a data
subject right under the Act; it cannot be a support ticket.

## Retention

Every consent record carries `retention_until`. Past that date the clip stops loading. Purpose
limitation and retention limits are both statutory requirements, not project preferences.

## Minors

Participants under 18 require a guardian consent record referencing the guardian's name and
relationship. The registry rejects a minor's record without one.

## What must never be committed

`data/raw/`, `data/annotated/`, `data/consent/` and `data/derived/` are git-ignored. Consent records
contain names and contact details; landmark files are biometric-adjacent data derived from
identifiable people. **Neither belongs in version control**, including in a private repository,
because git history makes deletion-on-withdrawal effectively impossible.

Sample data under `data/samples/` is synthetic — generated by
`signsync.datasets.synthetic`, derived from no real person — and is safe to commit.

## Still required outside this repository

Code cannot discharge these, and Phase 0 owns them (plan §16.1):

- Registration with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) under NITA-U as data controller.
- A Data Protection Impact Assessment, completed **before** collection begins.
- A consent process delivered **in USL by a fluent signer**, not an English form read aloud
  (plan §16.2).
- A breach notification plan, in place before the first recording.
- Cross-border transfer safeguards if any training touches cloud infrastructure outside Uganda.
